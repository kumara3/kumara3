## About Me

Computational biologist with 9+ years of experience in large-scale genomic data analysis, single-cell multi-omics integration, and machine learning driven discovery. Expert in designing reproducible, scalable pipelines for next-generation sequencing, variant discovery, and transcriptomic analysis across human and model systems. Strong background in computer science, algorithm design and computational analysis of complex biological systems. 

I prioritize mentoring junior team members while effectively leading senior staff, fostering a collaborative culture that builds strong team and support revenue growth.
I’m always open to exploring new opportunities and discussing how my skills can add value to your organization.
